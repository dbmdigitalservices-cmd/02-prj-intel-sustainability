Intel's Sustainability Journey

Explore the milestones that have shaped Intel's approach to sustainability.

1968

Intel Founded

Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore rename NM Electronics to Intel Corporation.

This decision lays the foundation for decades of technological innovation.

1971

First Microprocessor

Intel debuts the 4004, the world's first commercial microprocessor.

The 4004 ignites the microprocessor revolution and changes computing forever.

1978

8086 Processor

The 8086 processor establishes the x86 architecture.

This architecture goes on to drive countless PCs and servers.

1985

386 Processor

Intel introduces the 386 processor with 32-bit architecture.

The chip brings better performance and multitasking to personal computers.

2006

Peak GHG Emissions

Intel reaches its highest annual greenhouse gas emissions for operations.

The company responds with investments in abatement, renewable energy, and efficient manufacturing.

2020

RISE Strategy

Intel launches its Responsible, Inclusive, Sustainable, Enabling strategy.

The 2030 goals focus on climate action, water stewardship, and waste reduction.

2022

Net-Zero By 2040

Intel commits to net-zero Scope 1 and 2 emissions across global operations.

The commitment builds on years of environmental initiatives.

2023

Renewable Electricity

Intel achieves 99% renewable electricity usage worldwide.

This helps lower carbon emissions and advances its long-term goals.

2024

Sustainability Summit

Intel hosts its first Sustainability Summit.

Suppliers, officials, and industry leaders collaborate on sustainable manufacturing.
